## Sockrocket v0.1.15

- Expand the built-in CN IPv6 list to 2,314 prefixes. Classify each address in mixed DNS answers separately, expire DNS hints with the answer and preserve device, port and IP rules in TUN.
- Apply Merlin device rules to IPv6 addresses identified through a current IPv4 lease and a matching LAN neighbor. Recover IPv6 forwarding and DNS interception after a delegated prefix changes.
- Keep UDP sessions usable after empty or oversized packets. Support TCP retries of truncated DNS replies through TUN, and try other UDP-capable members during fallback group setup.
- Allow new TUN traffic to arrive during a blocked write. Use MTU-sized UDP receive buffers and share compiled rule groups across devices.
- Start direct TCP connections as each address family becomes available and try another UDP address after a local setup failure.
