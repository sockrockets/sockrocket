## Sockrocket v0.1.6

- In dual-stack mode, the Merlin CN bypass now sends connections to domestic IPv6 destinations directly through the router. Devices with matching routing rules still use TUN.
- Refreshing device rules cleans up the temporary kernel address sets on Merlin.
- The Merlin installer verifies that the Software Center page is present and registered. The settings page remains usable when firmware navigation is unavailable.
